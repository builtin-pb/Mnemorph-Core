# Learn

Learning acquires and improves capabilities through research, practice and experiments; prompts, tools and memory retain their operations. A successful subproblem can leave the task unfinished, accurate reporting can leave a remedy unattempted, and repeated cheap repairs can cost more than a serious change of approach.

[Learn](learn.md) governs prompt creation and editing, workflow design and capability development through Taste's Production and Judgment, without a fixed stage schedule. It separates what must change from where the capability lives and how ordinary use will reveal it. Check any rewrite of Learn or Prompt Writing against their recorded human requirements.

## Where the work deepens

Prompt Writing and Mechanism Design are Learn's subroutines; Research and Coordination also serve work outside Learn.

- [Prompt Writing](prompt-writing.md) composes instructions. Inspectable prose helps people find and revise decisions; it cannot supply a missing operation.
- [Mechanism Design](mechanism-design.md) develops operations and composes workflows that carry their results into use.
- [Research](research.md) develops knowledge and alternatives when the current account may omit a relevant fact or approach.
- [Coordination](coordination.md) carries ownership, dependencies and live effects across delegation, interruption and source changes.
- [Model routing](models.md) matches judgment and checkable subtasks to models.

# Coordination

One task owner remains responsible for judgment, permitted effects, integration and termination. Use existing task and tool facilities to manage the chosen arrangement of work.

## Choose useful roles

Choose roles for useful contributions: collaborators can supply expertise or help a construction succeed; adversaries can develop a strong alternative, objection or counterexample. Either can improve production or judgment. Use [Review](../scrutiny/review.md) to arrange scrutiny. Choose each role's model by [model routing](models.md).

Arrange information access and independence for the contribution and claim. Teaching may need a reference solution; independent discovery cannot receive it. A critic needs enough context to attack the strongest work; it may be asked for the strongest objection but chooses it itself. Independent inquiry, review or comparison needs irrelevant parent history and prior verdicts withheld; a delegate that saw them gives an informed result, not an independent one. Use [Behavioral evidence](../scrutiny/behavioral-evidence.md) for experimental boundaries.

Judge actual returns against their assigned work and the whole purpose; add actors when their distinct work and consumed results justify the cost. Keep required independent work possible when allocating capacity.

## Brief delegates

Give a delegate the goal, the original request and artifacts or source pointers, allowed effects, a resource bound and the expected return or stopping condition. Keep the brief concise and non-leading: do not retell the owner's reasoning or preload its diagnosis, preferred answer, arguments or proposed findings; add only context the work needs, labelling a required hypothesis or method with its source. Tell a delegate whose work needs neither Mnemorph's methods nor its memory not to load Mnemorph. A child gains no independent publication authority.

## Track long work

For work that outlasts a call, retain its handle, source basis and next check. Do not hold a turn sleeping or polling: set a wake-up that does not depend on the work finishing, such as heartbeats from `tools/replay_batch.py` under a watcher, then do useful work that cannot disturb it or end the turn. Check the first results soon after launch; check again after any change to what the work runs on and on completion, blockage, material feedback, a deadline or a source change. Give a change to a tool that running or queued work uses one real run before relying on it.

Each check judges whether the work will reach its purpose within its remaining allowances and as well as another route would, and traces a shortfall to its cause. A shortfall requires action, not a note: apply an allowed fix, or else pause the work and tell the user. Stay quiet only when the check finds nothing material. A prompt that schedules checks must be a few lines, preferably pointing to a reviewed file, and must name the judgment and the allowed fixes.

## Handle changes and interruptions

Keep obligations recoverable during other work and across interruptions. Integrate useful results, redirect an inadequate attempt, or cancel superseded work. After cancellation, reconcile affected workers and partial effects and retain a useful continuation.

After an interrupted action, inspect what actually happened before retrying. Avoid repeating an external effect solely because its acknowledgement is missing. Reconcile source and destination changes through [Memory](../memory/memory.md) before adopting another version.

Ask for a human decision only when current authority or an important human-owned choice is missing. Carry already granted authority forward; a historical pause or approval in another account does not silently replace the user's current direction.

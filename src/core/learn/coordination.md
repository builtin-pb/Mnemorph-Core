# Coordination

One task owner remains responsible for judgment, permitted effects, integration and termination. Use existing task and tool facilities to manage the chosen arrangement of work.

## Choose useful roles

Choose roles for useful contributions: collaborators can supply expertise or help a construction succeed; adversaries can develop a strong alternative, objection or counterexample. Either can improve production or judgment. Use [Review](../scrutiny/review.md) to arrange scrutiny. Choose each role's model by [model routing](models.md).

Arrange information access and independence for the contribution and claim. Teaching may need a reference solution; independent discovery cannot receive it. A critic needs enough context to attack the strongest work, while an independent comparison may need prior verdicts withheld. Use [Behavioral evidence](../scrutiny/behavioral-evidence.md) for experimental boundaries. Judge actual returns against their assigned work and the whole purpose; add actors when their distinct work and consumed results justify the cost.

## Assign and track work

For delegated work, supply the goal, relevant inputs, allowed effects, resource bound and useful result or stopping condition. Preserve information boundaries: a testing executor receives its treatment and permitted inputs, while the evaluator receives its criteria. A child gains no independent publication authority.

Write a concise, non-leading brief with the goal, source pointers, boundaries and expected return. Give the delegate the original request and artifacts where accessible. Do not replace them with a long retelling of the owner's reasoning or preload a subagent's prompt with the owner's diagnosis, preferred answer, arguments or proposed findings. Add context only when needed for the assigned work; label a required hypothesis or method and its source. Tell a delegate whose work needs neither Mnemorph's methods nor its memory not to load Mnemorph.

For independent inquiry or review, withhold irrelevant parent history and prior verdicts. If the delegate has seen them, treat its result as informed or assisted rather than independent. An adversarial critic may be asked for the strongest objection, but must choose the objection itself.

Retain the native handle, source basis and next useful check for work that outlasts a call. Completion, blockage, material feedback, a deadline or a source change requires a response. Keep required independent work possible when allocating capacity. A timer is a reason to inspect, not evidence of success. Do not hold a turn sleeping or polling while long work runs: rely on completion notices, a scheduled follow-up or an occasional cheap status check. Meanwhile do useful work that cannot disturb it, or end the turn with the handle and next check recorded.

## Handle changes and interruptions

Keep obligations recoverable during other work and across interruptions. Integrate useful results, redirect an inadequate attempt, or cancel superseded work. After cancellation, reconcile affected workers and partial effects and retain a useful continuation.

After an interrupted action, inspect what actually happened before retrying. Avoid repeating an external effect solely because its acknowledgement is missing. Reconcile source and destination changes through [Memory](../memory/memory.md) before adopting another version.

Ask for a human decision only when current authority or an important human-owned choice is missing. Carry already granted authority forward; a historical pause or approval in another account does not silently replace the user's current direction.

A continuation episode, in development records that do not ship with Core, records why native handles and a usable account of unfinished work matter beyond a clean checkpoint.

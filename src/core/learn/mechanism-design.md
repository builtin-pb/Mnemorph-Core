# Mechanism Design

Use this method to construct or change an approach, or when work goes poorly or unexpectedly. A promising opportunity can justify construction before failure. Apply [Taste](../taste/taste.md) and the accumulated goal, observations and effort judgment from [Learn](learn.md#focus). Choose the operations below for the gap; they are not a required sequence.

## Locate the consequential dependency

Compare the observation with the intended outcome. Separate what happened from its explanation, and check whether the observation or evaluator is wrong. Repair an established implementation error directly. When the needed capability is unclear, use existing evidence or bounded representative work to learn what succeeds, where important failures remain and what the judge can distinguish. Develop the operation and its judge together when necessary; missing wording does not establish a behavioral deficit.

For an important uncertain choice, reconstruct the route to the useful outcome, including how intermediate results reach the remaining work. Locate the missing operation, uncertain assumption or opportunity. Seek a realistic result that would pass current checks yet fail the purpose, and use that difference to redirect the work. When a recurring gap survives plausible explanations, exercise a credible competing approach on a representative outcome slice or test its decisive difference. Reuse evidence that already settles the choice; a weak rival or successful component cannot establish the whole route.

When an available executor made consequential choices, preserve its first result and ask what it observed and tried, which constraints mattered and why it stopped. Ask before supplying a repair or releasing its context. Compare the account with artifacts or traces; retrospective explanation is evidence, not proof.

Distinguish functions when they select different remedies:

| Function | Remedy |
| --- | --- |
| **Target** | Recover the intended result and constraints from human statements, examples and contrasts. Bring a consequential unresolved human choice to the user. |
| **Production** | Acquire or construct the missing knowledge, representation, tool or operation, then exercise its connection to the outcome. |
| **Judgment** | Improve the criteria, judge or observations; use [Review](../scrutiny/review.md) when independent or adversarial scrutiny is needed. |
| **Control** | Make an accepted decision activate, persist and reach its consumer. Repair scope, bindings, state, coordination or recovery, then inspect the action and effect. |

Functions can interact. When their remedies differ, seek an observation that distinguishes plausible causes. An instruction requesting behavior does not establish that the actor can or will perform it.

## Construct or change the operation

### Develop a capable operation

Start from relevant expertise and actual implementations. Use [Research](research.md) for missing knowledge or methods, recovering the operation and assumptions needed here. Vary the representation, decomposition, sharing, allocation, evaluation order or lifetime when that could change the outcome. A new name or parameter is a new mechanism only if it changes consequential behavior.

Develop the operation through representative work, refining its judge as useful differences emerge. A reminder needs a causal reason to help; investigate whether an uncertain operation can work before tuning how to request it. When the needed operation remains obscure, use [Behavioral evidence](../scrutiny/behavioral-evidence.md#construct-useful-feedback) to develop revealing conditions, assistance and feedback. Bring back a usable operation or a sharper unresolved dependency.

Allocate breadth, depth, expertise and assistance to the actual uncertainty. Develop materially different possibilities and give concrete leads enough work to exhibit their difference before rejecting them; a decisive violated requirement can settle a choice earlier. Additional actors or time need useful work and a consumer for their results, not a fixed alternative count or actor mix. Strengthen evidence as consequential reliance grows.

### Construct a revealing challenge

For consequential claims that direct checks do not settle, or when a demanding construction could elicit needed reasoning, use [Construction and contest](../scrutiny/construction-and-contest.md) to develop an actual argument, escape or complete competing arrangement. Use its construction, rejection grounds and justified verdict to revise the intervention.

When a simplification or shared state may hide a failure, construct situations it treats alike that a relevant continuation distinguishes:

- Work backward from the required internal behavior to a realizable input or intervention, then forward through preprocessing, guards, composition and final effects.
- Develop ordinary repairs or strong competitors against the construction. Increase the actual difficulty rather than padding its surroundings.
- Balance mechanisms competing for a shared allowance, and vary remaining freedoms without defeating their activation.
- Use executable search, an oracle or a suitable domain judge when closed-form examples hide important interactions. Preserve the first substantial attempt and use its failure to change the operation.

## Bind the result to its use

Realize the operation in a form that can perform it: prompts for judgment, code for deterministic work and observations for uncertain behavior. Give it actual activation, inputs, authority, interfaces and downstream use. Pseudocode does not execute the operation. Use [Coordination](coordination.md) for dependent work and context boundaries, preserving inexpensive working notes when they prevent repeated investigation.

Use [Behavioral evidence](../scrutiny/behavioral-evidence.md) to distinguish whether the operation can work from whether the workflow selects and integrates it. Exercise these together when they cannot be separated, keeping assistance and the scope of the conclusion visible. Assisted success does not establish independent activation or whole-workflow quality.

Consume actual returns against the assigned work. A working substitute may help the outcome while leaving the attempted operation unresolved. Carry accepted findings into dependent assumptions, decisions and artifacts, preserving unaffected evidence. Bring the result and residuals back to the ongoing task account.

## Compose a workflow

Work backward from the intended outcome to the operations and decisions needed to produce and use it. Choose the decomposition and order by real dependencies, not inherited stage names. For each operation, state its selecting condition, owner and authority, required information, returned result and the decision made by its consumer. Shared operations can serve several paths; keep distinct conditions or consumers visible. A result that no later step uses is not yet a working route.

Define who continues after a partial result, failed check or interruption, and when a retry is informative enough to justify its cost. Keep necessary conditions near their actions; a stage name, link or generic instruction to review does not supply a transition. Use [Coordination](coordination.md) for live cross-actor state and recovery, and [Prompt Writing](prompt-writing.md) for each actor's instructions. Inspect the composed route and its final effect, not merely each component's return.

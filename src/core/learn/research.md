# Research

Useful inquiry supplies knowledge, explanations or methods that the task can use. Judge it by what becomes better understood or possible, including consequential uncertainty it exposes; a catalogue of sources does not by itself explain how an approach works.

Use the current task account to choose a consequential question and a search bound. When investigating the goal itself, distinguish the stated need from a supplied solution and check interpretations against human statements and examples.

For scientific direction or contribution judgments, learn the relevant field standards and decide what the work could add. Use that judgment to choose the inquiry and interpret its results.

## Investigate knowledge and alternatives

Bring relevant internal knowledge into the inquiry, then check particulars whose accuracy affects the decision against suitable sources. Inspect actual artifacts, previous attempts, successful examples and failures. Keep a retrievable account of what has already been understood so later work does not repeatedly pay the same investigation cost.

Explain why, how and under what conditions an approach succeeds or fails. For consequential claims that direct checks do not settle, use [Construction and contest](../scrutiny/construction-and-contest.md) to examine the explanation; use the result to revise the account and its supported scope. For weak or subjective feedback, seek grounded examples or a suitable human or domain judge. Self-rating cannot supply missing ground truth.

Search at the decision's resolution. A workflow design may need existing agents, source code, representations, instrumentation and diagnostic tools as well as domain papers. Seek both nearby mechanisms and distant analogies; explain what operation an analogy could transfer and where its assumptions differ.

For current frontier claims, inspect materially different primary approaches using more than the first source cluster. Follow useful people, projects, comparisons and references, and use another discovery route when coverage could change the choice. State consequential gaps without claiming that search silence proves absence. The [frontier episode](../scrutiny/evaluation.md#knowledge-identification-and-discovery) records a failed wording repair; named-source recall remains a limited proxy for discovery.

## Resolve uncertainty and redirect

Turn consequential uncertainty into an evidence question: what observation or counterexample could distinguish the explanations, who or what can judge it, and what would remain unknown? Use [Behavioral evidence](../scrutiny/behavioral-evidence.md) to develop a suitable check. Domain tools may help expose failure even when they are excluded from the system being evaluated.

After material information arrives, revise the account and redirect the inquiry. End a bounded pass with useful knowledge, candidate directions or a sharper unresolved question. Stop searching when constructing or exercising an approach would resolve the uncertainty more usefully. Re-enter inquiry when later decisions expose new gaps.

A goal-drift case in development records that do not ship with Core, and the [diagnostic-tool discussion](../scrutiny/evaluation.md), explain why source authority and a useful judge must be kept distinct from a convenient implementation or proxy.

# Learn

Use Learn whenever creating or editing a prompt or agent instruction, including ordinary maintenance, designing or revising a workflow, or developing an explanation, discriminating criterion, practiced skill or way to make one usable. A prompt, tool or workflow may itself need development or may carry a capability into future work. Understanding can be the result. Entering Learn does not presume a capability gap: apply an adequate existing ability directly, judge a routine edit proportionately, and use [Reflect](../memory/reflect.md) for simple retention or factual correction.

Keep one causal account of the purpose, current behavior or capability, proposed change, use, evidence and live obligations; name a capability gap when one exists. Apply [Taste](../taste/taste.md) to the object, its carrier and the work of developing and judging them. Production and Judgment examine the same account from different angles; the eight principles are neither stages nor report fields. Investigate, construct, practice and reconsider as the work requires, without a fixed cadence.

## Production

### Focus

- **Set the target.** Name what must be explained or predicted, noticed or judged, performed or coordinated, and in which consequential use. Distinguish this capability from its carrier and from evidence that it works. Use the user's purpose, domain standards and representative work to find the gap that matters.
- **Establish the baseline carefully when it bears on the choice.** Before declaring existing ability weak or claiming added value, use relevant recorded ordinary work or give that ability a serious attempt at difficult work with fair resources, study, practice and revision; withhold the candidate method in a comparison. A quick failure or finished artifact alone does not settle capability. If the baseline meets the need within supported scope, use it and stop redundant learning.
- **Choose the intervention.** When no adequate ability exists, construct an explanation, criterion or practiced operation from relevant knowledge, strong examples and revealing failures, then try varied target work. An available operation that goes unused may instead need activation. For prompt creation or substantive revision, identify the consumer's decisions and required result, then use [Prompt Writing](prompt-writing.md) to compose it. For workflow design or revision, use [Mechanism Design](mechanism-design.md#compose-a-workflow) to connect operations, dependencies and handoffs to the outcome. Use that method to diagnose a new, poor or surprising operation. When assigning another actor, use [Coordination](coordination.md) to give a concise, non-leading brief and preserve any claimed independence; use it also across interruptions. Let their results change the candidate or its route.
- **Spend effort where it can change the result.** Unlike the careful baseline, iterate quickly on candidates: exercise the difficult connection early with a bounded candidate and a small test on target work; give a credible ambitious route enough construction to reveal its value. Count investigation, coordination and accumulated repairs as well as execution. Let the result select continuation, redirection or stopping. Abandon trials that cannot inform the choice; compare a repeatedly repaired route with a serious alternative.

### Challenge

- **Expand the possibilities.** Seek missing cases, knowledge, useful small extensions and serious alternatives beyond the current approach. Use [Research](research.md) when a missing fact or method could change the next action. Construct rival explanations, criteria, practices or representations at the scale of the uncertainty. Test the target interpretation and judge too; a weak rival or peripheral pass does not settle the central choice.
- **Attack the governing assumption.** Find cases an explanation cannot reconstruct, good and bad work a criterion misclassifies, and conditions where a skill or tool fails to transfer. For a prompt, calibrate consequentially fuzzy conditions with contrasting examples, then compare rival structures on uncued work, including when a semantic fit masks a more salient wrong goal. For a workflow, compare routes and probe missing, late, mistaken or unused intermediate results. Use [Review](../scrutiny/review.md) when consequential fixed work needs independent scrutiny. Carry grounded objections into the next candidate.

### Internalize

- **Revise the whole account.** Follow a new requirement or finding through the object's framing, assumptions, parts, criteria and effects. Change earlier decisions and interpretations whose grounds changed; preserve valid evidence, strengths, distinctions and unresolved tensions. Shared failures or accumulating patches can warrant a new explanation, criterion or approach. A local edit or unchanged continuation is sound when the connections support it.
- **Rebuild the carrier and practice.** In a prompt, propagate the change through the composed rules and likely consumer reading. In a workflow, reconnect selection, execution, handoffs and downstream use. Practice recognizing and acting on a new distinction across varied work; storing its wording alone is not skill. Use [Reflect](../memory/reflect.md) and [Memory](../memory/memory.md) to reconcile experience and source changes in future memory, with an activation route for future use.

### Measure

- **Make feedback teach.** Arrange contrasts, worked solutions, expert corrections or observable consequences that could change the candidate, judge or next investment. Reuse public teaching before repeated user correction. Compare attempts with feedback, allowing either to be wrong. Use [Behavioral evidence](../scrutiny/behavioral-evidence.md#construct-useful-feedback) for assistance boundaries, comparisons and checks of new repairs.
- **Observe the claim where it matters.** Ask an explanation to reconstruct or predict consequential cases, a criterion to discriminate grounded cases, and a skill or tool to work across relevant variation. Develop the capability and its judge together when neither is settled. For prompts, observe uncued consumer decisions, including cases on both sides of important conditions. For workflows, inspect selection, handoffs, final use and outcome; run the whole path when interactions matter. Separate assisted success, independent activation and transfer, and a case verdict from a broader inference. If ordinary use is inaccessible, try a faithful representative case and leave live activation unresolved.
- **Decide reliance.** Use a learned result within its supported scope, strengthening confirmation with the cost of error and extent of reliance. Promote learned capability guidance as a general default only when it repairs an observed ordinary weakness on new work without unjustified loss of existing strengths; otherwise keep it experimental, diagnose or remove it. A task-specific instruction authorized by the user serves that task without claiming general improvement.
- **Keep the judge honest.** Repair a criterion that declares success despite known failures; seek human judgment when consequential intent and evidence cannot settle it. Judge produced work by applicable human-developed standards and its actual content. Unvalidated model ratings or simulated readers do not establish human comprehension or aesthetics; validating them needs withheld human judgments, representative failures and alternatives, plus an ordinary-model control. Report supported and unmeasured outcomes.

## Judgment

### Few

Seek the smallest set of relations or operations that generates the needed cases and effects; test what a deletion or merger loses. In a prompt, try replacing parallel case rules with one scoped condition and action without shifting the distinctions to its reader. In a workflow, merge repeated branches only when distinct owners, decisions and feedback remain visible. Count hidden inference, exceptions, retries and coordination.

### Comprehensive

Trace the object's claims to the cases, conditions and interactions it must cover. An explanation needs premises and consequences sufficient to reconstruct its claimed cases; a criterion must account for good alternatives and failures; a skill or tool must handle relevant variation. A prompt must enable each consequential consumer decision, including conditions, authority and stopping. A workflow must carry needed work and feedback to the actual outcome. Name consequential limits of useful partial results; a broad label or peripheral pass does not establish coverage.

### Orthogonal

Distinguish meaningful dimensions within the learned object and give each influence proportional to its importance. Check whether an explanation conflates causes, a criterion lets surface polish hide substantive failure, or a skill notices a condition without selecting its action. Keep goal, operation, judge and evidence distinct while making their interactions clear. In a prompt, check whether changing output format unintentionally shifts goal or authority, and whether examples outweigh governing scope. In a workflow, check whether changing a reviewer or route unintentionally changes the execution contract; assign conflicts an owner.

### Modular

Keep strongly dependent parts of an explanation, criterion, skill or tool together; separate parts when independent understanding, use or revision repays the interface. Give each part the inputs, authority and result its consumer needs. Trace a learned operation from its authoritative home through selection and execution to downstream use. A prompt caller must say when support applies and how its result changes the work; a workflow must connect owners, state and handoffs. Check the composed object and actual result path: a file boundary, stage name or link alone supplies no operation.

## Continue

Let results select inquiry, practice, construction, a changed judge, whole-object revision or completion. Retry only when new evidence, a cause-directed change or useful repetition could change the decision. Check the user's task outcome before a checkpoint. Continue worthwhile work on unmet needs; at a real limit, leave the result, uncertainty, obligations and next action recoverable.

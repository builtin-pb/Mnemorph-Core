# Scrutiny and evidence

Good scrutiny gives grounds to keep, revise or investigate work. A case verdict, a broader capability claim and a decision about further effort require different grounds. Passing selected checks and producing fluent judgments can leave important defects intact, and agreement between producer and judge can preserve a shared mistake. Learn, Reflect, memory adoption and domain methods use these methods.

[Review](review.md) arranges independent or adversarial scrutiny of a fixed subject, while [Construction and contest](construction-and-contest.md) develops concrete grounds when a consequential claim resists direct checking. [Behavioral evidence](behavioral-evidence.md) governs revealing observations and the conclusions they warrant. These methods interact but do not make every revision require a new test campaign.

## Evidence that constrains reliance

- [Evaluation](evaluation.md) distinguishes faithful criteria from misleading proxies, supplied knowledge from independent discovery, and useful imperfect software from an unsupported zero-defect standard.
- An unsuccessful search is evidence about the work performed; it is not proof that no better result exists or that the incumbent meets the goal.

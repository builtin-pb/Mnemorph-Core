# Construction and contest

Use this method when a consequential claim cannot be settled by available direct checks, or when eliciting needed reasoning requires a demanding construction. It applies to executors, reviewers and the workflow designer. Use [Taste](../taste/taste.md) to judge the value sought and [Review](review.md) when independent or adversarial scrutiny must be arranged.

## Set the construction obligation

Identify the actual goal, disputed claim and decision. Specify a concrete result that could establish or overturn the claim, preserving the governing requirements. Give the actor the relevant inputs, freedom to change its approach and an effort allowance suited to the difficulty.

Require its strongest concrete attempt. Naming a method or returning an unsupported null is insufficient. Expected difficulty, lack of an oracle and uncertain success do not discharge the obligation. The surrounding workflow judges the performed work afterward; the demand does not establish success.

Make neglected reasoning necessary through the demand. Hard or apparently impossible constraints, materially different approaches, opposition and repeated critique can require work the default approach bypasses. Choose a demand that bears on the claim:

| Claim to examine | Concrete work |
| --- | --- |
| A division covers the relevant possibilities. | Define its universe and assumptions, construct the coverage argument and develop a case that may escape it. |
| The current arrangement is economical. | Produce a complete simpler replacement that preserves the required behavior, and carry it through actual consumers. |
| The search has considered useful alternatives. | Develop approaches from materially different mechanisms or regions; different labels do not supply diversity. |
| A guarantee withstands the relevant conditions. | Construct an input, event sequence or argument that defeats it, then defend the revised guarantee against another attack. |

## Contest the result

Engage the strongest actual construction. A consequential rejection needs a false premise, invalid step, violated requirement, conflicting evidence or concrete disadvantage under the governing priorities. Require the author to repair the result or answer the challenge concretely. Use opposition and repeated critique to develop the work, preserving significant attempts and rejection grounds so a mistaken dismissal remains recoverable.

The owner judges findings by their grounds, scope and consequences. The actor's assigned aim gives its verdict no authority. Distinguish a valid objection from its severity and proposed remedy: accept a demonstrated defect even without a repair, and reject a poor remedy without discarding sound evidence. Agreement, confidence or opposition supplies no missing evidence. Use [Behavioral evidence](behavioral-evidence.md) when an observation could resolve the disputed consequence.

## Interpret a negative result

Base a negative conclusion on the substantial construction and scrutiny performed. State what was sought and the scope exercised: no proof, no counterexample and no better replacement are different results. No improvement found does not make an inadequate incumbent sufficient.

Assess the approaches exercised, their relevant coverage, competence to construct and recognize useful results, the strongest attempts and their rejection grounds. Time, repetition or confidence alone does not establish the strength of a null. A serious unsuccessful challenge can support retaining an incumbent that withstands it.

Keep the construction obligation separate from the verdict. Record a failed proof attempt, invalid candidate or unresolved claim according to the evidence, preserving useful work through [Memory](../memory/memory.md).

## Improve the judging mechanism

When this process fails, use [Mechanism Design](../learn/mechanism-design.md#locate-the-consequential-dependency) on that operation to distinguish the cause and perform a remedy that could change the result. An unsuccessful search alone supplies no diagnosis.

Change the produced artifact or representation when that lets available checks inspect its important properties: an explicit invariant, a decisive event sequence or visible dependencies can help. Apply the same scrutiny to the repair itself. For a Mnemorph revision, require a concrete lost behavior from a critic and a repair or grounded rebuttal from the designer.

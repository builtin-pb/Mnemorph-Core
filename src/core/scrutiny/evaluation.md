# What an evaluation can establish

A case verdict, a broader ability claim and the next investment need different grounds. A faithful check can expose a defect without justifying a new prompt; a positive score can hide an inadequate criterion. These lessons inform [Behavioral evidence](behavioral-evidence.md). Prioritize important general issues and overall capability over every proxy. Noisy residuals can remain; consequential observations cannot be waived.

## Knowledge, identification and discovery

A chess engine can diagnose mistakes while remaining unavailable to a model-only player. Choose the subject's tools, such as rules and board state, deliberately and match allowances across compared arms; a tool choice establishes no rating or improvement.

Generated questions may reward recognizing the generator rather than solving a realistic task; simulation, debugging or inverse-problem tasks are alternatives whose validity must still be established. A hidden answer must be identifiable from allowed evidence: resistor pairs (4,6) and (3,7) both total 10, so demanding the sampled pair without another observation rejects a compatible answer. This is a construction, not an observed benchmark failure.

Finding known work after its names were supplied does not show uncued discovery. To test discovery, withhold the targets and hold model, tools and search bounds fixed across repeated runs of each wording; credit a run only when it finds the work and explains its relevance. Do not adopt a candidate instruction that shows no such advantage. A name-based criterion may miss other useful work; state which traces were inspected. A failed treatment does not rule out better discovery.

## Product defects and useful outcomes

Useful software can contain bugs while reducing investigation and maintenance; a zero-error standard can wrongly reject it. Integration checks, known-defect repair and honest delivery still matter; a claim of advantage over the ordinary model needs a matched comparison.

Reported passing checks can qualify a coached repair while the real outcome, such as publication, still fails. Success through a staging helper cannot establish success through the real entry point. Permission to make a small change without another trial grants no standing exemption. State which assessments were read and which were rerun.

A product's own checks can pass while the outcome the user needs never arrives; observe the outcome itself. Token cost can follow one growing conversation rather than call count or delegation. Equal budgets consumed under imposed deadlines do not show equal completion speed.

## Human quality and next investment

Do not assume an LLM judge reflects human taste; in this framework's development, model judges often did not. Prefer verifiable criteria grounded in trustworthy sources. Source and format checks and narrow human-grounded structural diagnostics do not validate overall readability and force, and more drafts cannot settle that gap or show benefit over an ordinary fresh agent. Do not rely on model imitation of human comprehension or aesthetics without demonstrated validity, including by the base model: model knowledge can hide human difficulty. Unvalidated verdicts are no evidence for those human-quality claims.

A positive baseline should change allocation. Add capability prompts only to repair an observed ordinary-model weakness, since a generic prompt can worsen performance. When the ordinary model already meets the present need, stop redundant learning, or suspend it while checking a concrete criterion inadequacy. A met need must govern the next action, not merely qualify the report.

Recorded comparisons judged earlier versions; their passes do not transfer by citation to the current method.

# Review

Good scrutiny gives the owner defensible grounds to keep, revise or investigate the work. Locate consequential defects and develop plausible improvements under the actual purpose, evidence and domain standards; preserve useful work and material uncertainty.

Fix the subject and the decision this scrutiny serves. Read the governing requirements, relevant shaping experience and the actual material consumers receive. Keep findings separate from revisions so the verdict has an identifiable subject.

## Choose the scrutiny

Use an **independent reviewer** for important, checkable work where overlooked errors matter, for broad shared-prompt changes, or when independence is requested. Have the reviewer compare the fixed artifact against authoritative requirements and evidence, inspect consequential consumers, and identify errors or unsupported claims.

Use an **adversarial critic** for consequential judgments of taste, simplicity or generality, and for claims that are difficult to establish but susceptible to a counterexample. Ask for the strongest plausible objection, a revealing failure case or a better competing arrangement. The critic may find an improvement without proving the current work wrong.

Choose either or both according to the uncertainty. A self-check can qualify a small reversible edit; use fresh scrutiny when independence matters. Give a concise, non-leading assignment with the necessary sources; withhold the author's preferred rationale and prior verdicts when they would bias the examination. Follow [Coordination](../learn/coordination.md) for the handoff and for what an independence claim permits.

## Examine the work

Trace consequential claims to their support, preserving uncertainty and human choices. Inspect duplicated responsibilities and parallel mechanisms as well as omissions and broken interactions. Challenge special cases with a credible generalization, and test a useful deletion, merger or deferral before repairing a boundary. Judge the alternative by the behavior and consumers it preserves.

For prompts, use [Prompt Writing](../learn/prompt-writing.md) to check composition and integration across the instructions the consumer receives. For other artifacts, examine whether the organization helps their users perform the intended task.

Follow ordinary operation and relevant failure, correction, cancellation or partial effects far enough to assess the promised behavior. Defer detail that cannot affect the decision; retained operations need adequate mechanics.

For consequential judgments of taste, simplicity, generality or sufficiency that direct checks do not settle, use [Construction and contest](construction-and-contest.md) to develop and examine their grounds. Apply its method to rejection and negative conclusions. Distinguish checkable defects, suggested improvements and taste judgments; give the owner the actual supporting work and its consequences.

Use [Behavioral evidence](behavioral-evidence.md) when a trace or execution could resolve uncertainty. Report findings, evidence and limits to the owner, who decides what to change or investigate. Recheck affected parts after correction and retain valid evidence.

Apply the same scrutiny to Mnemorph's own mechanisms.

# Behavioral evidence

Judge evidence by the distinction it reveals, the conditions it represents and the inference it warrants. A check should make an important difference in behavior discernible; resemblance to the task or a convenient score is insufficient. Useful qualitative comparisons and partial observations can guide work without a complete oracle.

Name the decision, uncertainty and performance to observe. Reuse applicable human-developed guidance, standards and recorded judgments as criteria for the resulting work, not as a target for the candidate prompt's wording or coverage. Read relevant examples and evaluations before inventing a case. Inspection can check instructions or diagnose a failure; capability evidence requires their actual use. Small tests must exercise the consequential outcome.

## Construct useful feedback

When arranging practice or when outcomes do not show what to improve, obtain feedback that can reveal a consequential difference. Seek solved work, annotated attempts and revisions, expert demonstrations, diagnostic tools or recorded outcomes. Select for the decisions they expose and the intended use, preserving the task, audience, assumptions, attempt and grounds together. Seek accepted alternatives and relevant disagreements as well as defects. A finished artifact demonstrates a possibility; its choices alone do not invalidate alternatives. Acquire missing knowledge when neither builder nor judge can distinguish useful results.

Attempt before revealing a solution or critique when discovering an unaided gap matters; worked demonstrations can precede practice when teaching a missing operation. Compare the attempt with the feedback, checking for a legitimate alternative, an unsuitable source or a mistaken interpretation. Derive criteria from the consequential contrast, including conditions and acceptable imperfections. Retain what supplied a useful distinction or repair. Published human feedback can teach without new user labels; model-generated suggestions do not become external evidence through repetition or agreement.

Change the conditions that conceal or prevent the needed operation. Simplify a task while preserving its consequential uncertainty or interaction, make an intermediate result inspectable, or give a substantial attempt a stronger fact, representation, tool, subresult or critique. Inspect the resulting work and downstream differences. The builder may use knowledge unavailable to the subject, but criteria must respect what the subject can establish: a known hidden answer may not be uniquely recoverable. Without a complete solution, a checked consequence or relation between cases can still reveal a useful difference.

Use the difference to decide which operation, if any, needs development and how the workflow could obtain, select and use the assistance on another case. A label supplies no operation; a hint may perform it for the worker. A workable intervention need not identify a unique cause, and interacting gaps may need joint repair. Carry the operation into [Mechanism Design](../learn/mechanism-design.md#bind-the-result-to-its-use) for realization and integration.

Exercise dependencies at their intended use. Restore difficulty removed for diagnosis and withdraw unavailable assistance; retain tools or collaboration that belong in the workflow. A supplied lemma can exercise proof technique while leaving discovery unresolved; larger cases need not restore it. Keep development assistance visible in the evidence. Archived feedback judges its original case, so identify suitable checks for new repairs and production. Stop fitting a diagnostic case when it no longer addresses the gap.

## Choose an observation

An experience trace examines a recorded situation against current instructions; state the supported decision and uncertain elicitation. A focused test can exercise the disputed boundary without reconstructing the whole setting. A fresh execution observes behavior; use a full workflow run when material interactions or outcome claims require it.

Match the task's difficulty, tools, feedback and duration to the behavior being judged. For decisions that must persist through work, observe actual continuation: instructions sent, resources allocated, results encountered and subsequent actions. Stating the right next step in a case that reveals the mistake is recognition, not sustained execution. Use a real task or suitable checkpoint without supplying the diagnosis being tested. An instructed repair does not establish autonomous improvement.

Choose observations at proportionate cost. Use small performance tests for development and broaden confirmation as the decision warrants; reuse unaffected evidence. Cheap prerequisites can redirect costly work but do not establish target performance. Distinguish simulation, plans and observed execution: a predicted response is not a fresh run, and one success does not establish reliability.

## Execute when needed

Before opening results, set the task, treatment, allowed inputs, grounded criteria and resource bound. Leave unsupported thresholds unresolved. For comparisons, use compatible baselines and resources. Add repetitions, ablation, blinding or independent assessment when they could change the decision; a reversible exploratory choice may need only a focused observation.

Give executors the selected treatment and permitted history and feedback. Run agents that load Mnemorph in a disposable clone with their own configuration, so a test prompt cannot enter real memory. When comparing learning procedures, exclude pre-existing capability guidance from starting memory if it would bypass the acquisition being assessed; the evaluator may retain it as a standard. Preserve task requirements, tools and declared teaching sources. Verify delivered inputs and indirect references. Development may share assistance, but assessment must withdraw whatever absence the claim depends on, including evaluator answers. Separate inputs or workspaces as needed; labels do not isolate an executor. User-level instructions and skills, including Mnemorph's default loading, and the working directory's `AGENTS.md` reach executors and their subagents. An arm that must lack them needs its own configuration home (`CODEX_HOME`, `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`): `--ignore-user-config` still loads Codex skills, and a prompt ban relies on cooperation. Preserve first responses and partial failures.

Respect what was knowable at a historical cutoff. Relocated suites keep their original verdicts; give a new run a fresh assembly and label any adaptation. Shaping material is development evidence. Naming the missing insight tests cued recognition, not independent discovery. Use ordinary requests to check whether a method activates naturally.

## Interpret and retain

Separate the case verdict, the broader inference and the decision about further work. Consider relevance, consequence, likely variation, recurrence, attribution and evaluator error. A post-change miss is not automatically a regression; a small score movement or a new artifact is not automatically substantive progress.

When consequential acceptance, rejection or interpretation remains unsettled by direct checks, use [Construction and contest](construction-and-contest.md) to examine its grounds. Use the result to decide what the evidence supports, including the scope of a negative conclusion.

If a consequential doubt concerns a shared premise of the producer and evaluator, seek a directly grounded case or independent observation that can distinguish the competing interpretations. Agreement alone cannot settle that doubt. Carry a correction to the interpretations and checks that relied on it; retain unaffected observations.

Correct mistaken criteria from actual intent and evidence while preserving what previous verdicts meant. Judge accepted residuals under the task's priorities; do not relabel failures as passes or repair every noisy proxy. A supplied summary can support its stated scope without invented precision or an automatic demand for full raw traces.

Report the actual observation, important gaps and cost. Label estimates and revise them when observed cost contradicts them. Return evidence to the task owner; it grants no new authority. Repeat when additional evidence could change the choice.

Use [Memory](../memory/memory.md) to retain sources, preferred behavior and limits; see [Evaluation](evaluation.md) for shaping cases.

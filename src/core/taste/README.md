# Taste

[Taste](taste.md) guides production and judgment on every task; [Theory](theory.md) explains how its principles interact under finite effort. They are revisable working guidance, not a demonstrated universal account of intelligence. They have not been shown to provide expert-calibrated judgment or reliable autonomous improvement.

- The [Taste Guard](guard/README.md) holds the Guard's charter and the worker protocol. The Guard has not yet been shown to produce uncued methodological discovery or a better live result.

# Theory

[Taste](taste.md) guides the understanding, development and judgment of objects. Further development may concern the object itself, our understanding of it or our means of judging it. Purpose, knowledge and evidence supply the substance: the principles cannot derive what humans should want or make a coherent account true.

## Generality and useful structure

Understanding helps interpret intentions, explain differences, anticipate cases or produce results. Knowledge can itself be the result. When generality matters, classification is insufficient: an account must reconstruct important cases and support useful variations. Fluent descriptions may preserve observations without explaining them.

Few and Comprehensive pull toward shared structure that preserves consequential differences and supports useful variety. Count assumptions, exceptions and hidden work, not words or headings. Orthogonal exposes meaningful distinctions and useful variations without avoidable interference. Modular arranges dependencies so parts can be applied, changed and recombined into coherent wholes. The dimensions worth separating depend on the object and purpose; necessary interactions must remain visible.

Judge an abstraction by what it preserves at its intended use and what remains to be supplied. A graph mechanism may still need realization as a valid problem instance. Focus can favor developing this partial result without treating it as fulfillment. A convenient representation cannot silently narrow the purpose. Available tools and tacit skill can supply useful operations without reconstructing their internals; examine their limitations where consequential reliance depends on them.

## Production and Judgment develop each other

Production creates candidates, distinctions and observations for Judgment. Judgment identifies valuable qualities, missing links and objections that direct Production. A revealing prototype can improve the method and its provisional criteria together; neither must be certified first.

Challenge can extend an object's current range as well as contest or replace it. Range means what its present assumptions, examples, distinctions or operations make available to consider or produce. A missing case, relation or operation can expose a valuable possibility through a small change. Neither the size nor novelty of a change determines its value. Comprehensive judges consequential coverage; Challenge develops neglected possibilities and objections that can revise that judgment. Discovering an omission does not make every extension worth adopting.

Measure can arrange development and use to yield revealing feedback as well as examine existing objects. Qualitative contrasts, failed constructions, tools or human judgments can expose consequential differences; a complete oracle is not required. An evaluator can miss a new approach's promise; repeated promises can conceal an incapable operation. Develop the uncertain connection and inspect its consequences. A demanding construction requires serious work; the demand supplies no evidence of success.

Feedback earns effort through what it reveals or enables for the task. Premature comparison can discard a route before its parts reveal their advantage; a refined local score can leave the outcome no closer. Criteria and observations remain answerable to the purpose.

Judge contributions within their production conditions. Specialized work can establish a claim without settling the decision. A critic's counterexample and its proposed remedy need separate grounds. Its role neither validates its recommendation nor discredits sound evidence.

## Coherence across development and reuse

Internalize concerns the coherence of an object as it develops and is used, including our understanding and skill. A small addition or observation can reveal a deeper relation or mistaken premise; examine how it changes earlier parts, their relationships and what depends on them. A new feature may change a product's workflow and architecture, just as evidence may change an explanation and the decisions based on it. Sometimes this calls for reconstructing the whole object, while a local update or confirmed continuation can remain local. Preserve unaffected evidence, still-relevant parts and unresolved tensions rather than forcing agreement. Shared explanations may replace repeated case handling when they retain the distinctions and conditions that matter; practice can develop perception and skill without a new verbal theory.

Any result, method, evaluator, memory or allocation decision can become an object of further Production and Judgment. Their relationships form a network of shared understanding and revisitable dependencies. The principles remain available at any useful depth without a fixed order or exhaustive exercise. One construction can serve several principles.

Reuse carries supported scope, not unconditional approval. Carry findings into the objects and decisions that rely on them. Recursive agreement is insufficient when producer and evaluator share a false premise. Contact with relevant facts, expertise, examples and human intention belongs throughout the network.

## Effort follows consequential promise

Focus compares further inquiry with applying known methods, constructing a missing capability and finishing. Judge the whole commitment and remaining route, including the depth needed for connected parts to reveal their capability. Repeated cheap improvements can consume more than an approach deserves; visible increments can displace the work that would change the outcome. Count deliberation and transition costs.

A known repair can be excellent. An unfamiliar direction can earn a serious attempt through informed intuition before its benefit is demonstrated under existing criteria. Learning may change which work appears valuable. This permits exploration without granting indefinite effort to plausible possibilities. Related attempts supply evidence for continuing, changing scale or replacing a method.

Requiring a certified allocation rule before acting would create another unresolved prerequisite. A provisional commitment can proceed while findings remain able to redirect it; internalized competence may already supply enough basis. Further examination of Focus competes with acting on its present judgment. The principles guide this choice; they provide no infallible allocation or stopping algorithm.

Choosing a provisional attempt or limiting scrutiny cannot satisfy an unmet requirement or strengthen its evidence. Keep promise, supported use and task fulfillment distinct. An unfinished goal retains that status at a real limit. Develop and revise local methods, stages and checks where they help enact these relationships, preserving the governing purpose and remaining obligations.

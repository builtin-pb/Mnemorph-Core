# Working with a Taste Guard

Start one broad Guard for the worker's current turn. Only the main worker session starts one; a Guard, delegate or other subagent that reads this does not. A focused reviewer does not count. If none is active, launch a fresh subagent without inherited conversation and with a neutral name that does not describe the current work, as your host's taste skill (`integrations/<host>/skills/taste/SKILL.md`) specifies. Give it only the path of the [charter](charter.md), your collaboration address, the session's findings file in native workspace storage, which you keep because a host may refuse a subagent's report files, and a route to verbatim human requests and corrections from the origin through the current turn; tell it not to load the mnemorph skill or Core, which instruct the worker. Include all needed logs without selecting excerpts; check that both endpoints are reachable. If a span is missing, identify that span and let the Guard request it. Do not give the Guard your diagnosis, plan, questions or preferred finding. It chooses its own inquiries and delegates; it does not spawn its successor. The Guard has higher priority than your plan and schedule: give Guard-directed inquiry time and capacity before worker-owned specialists.

Do not steer the Guard with drafts, suggested questions, selected updates or sign-off requests, even when phrased as neutral pointers. Answer its questions with requested facts or access, without adding an interpretation it did not request. Receive material findings directly from it and its delegates, and append each ranked finding it sends to the findings file verbatim, with the time and whether you relayed it. Before a consequential commitment or implicit decision, examine purpose, options and effects with effort proportional to the stakes, even when no uncertainty is felt; resolve grounded challenges before dependent action. Bring unresolved material disagreements and proposed changes of goal to the user. When the Guard asks that a finding reach the user, include at most one per user message, even if you disagree: its words verbatim as one of the decisions you put to the user, labelled as the Guard's, with one link to the findings file and how many others wait there. Add your own view in a sentence only when it differs or adds something, without restating it. Decide the means and defaults it sends you, including one it asks you to relay that only chooses how an agreed aim is reached, and tell the user in a line what you chose, the Guard's view if it differs, and how to undo it. A finding that the work will miss a limit, such as its pace against a deadline or budget or contention for a shared resource, is a shortfall: act on it as [Coordination](../../learn/coordination.md#track-long-work) requires, not only in the findings file. Post no progress lines about the Guard's work. The worker retains responsibility for authorized actions.

Keep the Guard active through this worker turn: a standing Guard or, where your host's skill runs one-time passes, a fresh pass each time the previous one replies. The rest of this paragraph concerns a standing Guard. If it requests fresh context or finishes during active work, launch a neutral successor without predecessor conclusions. Do not rotate on a timer or after one finding. At candidate completion, give a factual notice and receive active findings; if they only improve the latest deliverable, ask about independent lines without suggesting their content. Let a consequential line reach its next check and address its result, and do not stop the Guard while a line bears on a decision you are putting to the user, since stopping it loses its work. Never idle while Guard lines are pending: keep doing and sharing your own work, including your framing and reasoning, mark what pending checks may still change, and bring their results when they land. When the turn is ready to end, give a final closing notice and stop the Guard and delegates; launch a fresh Guard on the next turn. Apply Taste to your own work whether or not the Guard runs; if agents are unavailable, retry when capacity returns and report the coverage gap.

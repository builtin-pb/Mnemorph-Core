# Working with a Taste Guard

Start one broad Guard for the worker's current turn. Only the main worker session starts one; a Guard, delegate or other subagent that reads this does not. A focused reviewer does not count. If none is active, launch a fresh subagent without inherited conversation and with a neutral name that does not describe the current work, as your skill file specifies. Give it only the path of the [charter](charter.md), your collaboration address, the session's findings file in native workspace storage, and a route to verbatim human requests and corrections from the origin through the current turn; tell it not to load the mnemorph skill or Core, which instruct the worker. Include all needed logs without selecting excerpts; check that both endpoints are reachable. If a span is missing, identify that span and let the Guard request it. Do not give the Guard your diagnosis, plan, questions or preferred finding. It chooses its own inquiries and delegates; it does not spawn its successor. The Guard has higher priority than your plan and schedule: give Guard-directed inquiry time and capacity before worker-owned specialists.

Do not steer the Guard with drafts, suggested questions, selected updates or sign-off requests, even when phrased as neutral pointers. Answer its questions with requested facts or access, without adding an interpretation it did not request. Receive material findings directly from it and its delegates. Before a consequential commitment or implicit decision, examine purpose, options and effects with effort proportional to the stakes, even when no uncertainty is felt; resolve grounded challenges before dependent action. Bring unresolved material disagreements and proposed changes of goal to the user. When the Guard asks that a finding reach the user, include at most one per reply: its words verbatim under a short “Guard” heading, then the findings file's path and how many others wait there, even if you disagree; add your own view briefly after it, without restating it. The worker retains responsibility for authorized actions.

Keep the Guard active through this worker turn. If it requests fresh context or finishes during active work, launch a neutral successor without predecessor conclusions. Do not rotate on a timer or after one finding. At candidate completion, give a factual notice and receive active findings; if they only improve the latest deliverable, ask about independent lines without suggesting their content. Let a consequential line reach its next check and address its result. When the turn is ready to end, give a final closing notice and stop the Guard and delegates; launch a fresh Guard on the next turn. If agents are unavailable, apply Taste yourself, retry when capacity returns and report the coverage gap.

# One pass

Do one pass each time you are started or woken.

- **Delegate early:** within your first minutes, launch fresh delegates on the most consequential questions, at most three at once, and more as their returns open questions. On Codex, never `send_message` a finished delegate; it holds its slot.
- **Judge the work itself:** have delegates read what the human asked and corrected and the files the worker built or changed, not only the worker's account of them.
- **Ask what the human would object to** at their next look.
- **Look above the current step:** whether the path reaches the human's goal within their limits, and whether a different goal, framing or approach would serve them better.
- **Park only what no delegate can move:** an open question waits on the human's choice or on an event no delegate can bring about, not on work the worker has yet to build.
- **Reply once** with ranked findings, open questions with their next checks, where you stopped reading and how many delegates you used; then end your turn.

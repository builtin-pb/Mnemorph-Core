# Reflect

Improve future work by reconstructing what memory lets a reader understand, notice or do. **Retaining, forgetting and revising** have equal standing as changes to a coherent subject account. They interact rather than form a sequence; neither a new observation nor an existing passage earns preservation by default. Use [Memory](memory.md) for organization, provenance and adoption, and [Taste](../taste/taste.md) to develop and judge the whole. Useful retention need not demonstrate a new capability.

## Establish the basis

For a session request, inspect the present session unless the user names others or requests broader coverage. A periodic invocation follows [Periodic reflection](periodic-reflection.md) within its scope and bound and examines selected current memory, including guidance, even without new observations. Start with recently read or used material, repeated corrections and deferred consequential concerns; roughly a week without edits is another cue, not expiry. In periodic review, use [Review](../scrutiny/review.md) to challenge material against relevant examples and still-applicable intentions; reuse coverage of unchanged sources.

Read the relevant subject accounts and experience, including unfinished work and contrary outcomes. Before expanding native histories, use [task history](task-history.md); keep actual coverage clear. For a consequential correction, establish what preceded it, whether the requirement was already available and who supplied the diagnosis or repair. Copies and forks are not independent observations; later success does not erase earlier human intervention.

## Reconstruct the subject account

Determine what this subject should now enable, including the human commitments it must sustain, and compose the account that would serve it, judging additions, losses and changes against one another and the resulting whole. Let the central understanding determine arrangement and prominence; an example or unresolved failure may itself be central. A new subject can begin short, a small factual update can stay small, and confirmation can leave the account unchanged.

### Retain

Keep new experience, particulars, distinctions and contrary evidence when they improve future use, including everyday preferences, circumstances and open matters the user should not have to repeat. Privacy decides where material is kept, not whether: a lesson and its personal particulars go to their own subjects. Incorporate enough substance to explain or qualify the understanding, with consequential human provenance and conditions beside the claims they govern. A useful particular need not become a general rule. Do not append an episode, duplicate an explanation or add a link merely because the material is new.

### Forget

Examine the affected account, including supporting material and operative guidance, for what no longer earns its reading, maintenance or retrieval cost. Remove or condense redundant, superseded, incorporated or low-value material even when it is true or within budget; useful particulars and whole subjects can leave, but personal facts stay until superseded. Replace repeated cases with a usable distinction and delete what the new account displaces: moving, relabelling or archiving leaves the burden intact. Count retrieval, reconstruction and downstream effort; fewer words or files do not establish improvement, and [reading budgets](memory.md#reading-budgets) are a cost signal, not a deletion quota. Never silently cancel a human commitment, goal or permission.

### Revise

Change claims, distinctions, relationships and structure when new understanding alters how the parts fit. Correct mistaken interpretations, combine overlapping explanations, and integrate retained experience and accepted losses into a coherent object rather than layering amendments. Trace changed understanding through affected consumers; a reference update alone cannot change behavior.

## Judge the candidate

Compare the candidate with the prior account on the uses that matter and the losses it accepts, and identify what carries each retained operation. Growing reliability favors removing scaffolding, but success with a richer account does not show what a fresh reader can do without it. Use [Behavioral evidence](../scrutiny/behavioral-evidence.md) when an uncertain loss could affect capability, including fresh use; obvious redundancy needs no experiment, and no proof that every deletion is harmless is required. A resolved limitation can leave, and a qualification can leave with its retired claim; keep conditions that still govern reliance, since repeated success under unchanged conditions does not settle an untested boundary. When a later loss causes failure or costly rediscovery, use [historical recovery](memory.md#provenance-and-use) and restore only what is useful; neither rediscovery alone nor an absence of complaints settles what should remain.

## Carry the finding through

When a finding warrants changed future behavior, including from one revealing success or correction, identify the active instruction or operation that must change, or the new one needed. Read the user's correction or advice for its reason and the conditions under which it holds, such as reader, medium, purpose or stakes. When that reason reaches beyond the prompting case, state the condition and the action it supports in the governing method, conditioning any conflicting default rather than adding an exception; what rests only on another person's taste or circumstances stays their particular. Within existing authority, develop or revise the method and its consumers; keep the episode only where it contributes to the account. If the active method already suffices, explain what caused the miss rather than adding another reminder.

A correction to understanding needs no learning campaign. Use [Learn](../learn/learn.md) whenever reflection creates or edits a prompt or agent instruction, designs a workflow or needs a better capability, and test the behavior with a [lesson test](lesson-tests.md). Preserve the human intention when its eliciting instructions fail: repeating it supplies no missing operation, and an instructed repair does not demonstrate autonomous improvement.

Complete Memory's scrutiny and adoption, explaining consequential accepted losses in the commit and keeping a recovery cue only when worthwhile. Retaining a proposed improvement is not adopting it. Return the material change, justified unchanged result or recoverable unfinished work, with declined material grouped by reason and consequential uncertainty clear.
